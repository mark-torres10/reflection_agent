Client’s goal: 

Launch MVP

We are a new kind of space for personal reflection and growth — what we call "a retreat in your pocket." Unlike typical AI tools, our product is built to be a calm, proactive facilitator that helps users feel deeply understood through reflection, grounding, and multi-day journeys.

The Role

We are looking for a specialist AI Engineer to lead the core technical challenge of our MVP: defining what is possible through custom model training and tuning.

The biggest unknown for our build is how to translate the expertise of human facilitators (retreat and workshop leaders) into a high-quality, emotionally-aware AI experience. You will be responsible for the "intelligence layer" that ensures our reflections are specific, perceptive, and entirely non-generic.

Key Responsibilities

Model Strategy & Feasibility: Determine the optimal approach for the MVP—whether that involves fine-tuning existing models, complex RAG, or custom training cycles.
Expert Knowledge Transfer: Work directly with human facilitators to capture their unique methodology and "train" the model to reflect their tone, pacing, and insight.
Prompt Architecture: Design and iterate on structured prompt/response systems that move beyond naive chat to provide high-fidelity, structured outputs.
Memory & Continuity: Develop the logic for how the AI manages conversation state and "lightweight memory" so user threads feel continuous and personal over time.
Tone & Quality Control: Rigorously iterate on model outputs to eliminate "assistant-like" or generic language, ensuring the AI feels like a perceptive, calm presence.
Technical Requirements

LLM Expertise: Deep, hands-on experience with LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source equivalents).
Fine-Tuning & Training: Proven track record of custom training or fine-tuning models for specific tones, domains, or behavioral outputs.
Structured Outputs: Mastery of prompt engineering for complex, structured data and multi-step conversational flows.
Data & Memory: Experience implementing memory layers and managing conversation state for long-term user engagement.
What We Care About Most

Reflection Quality: Can you make a model produce an output that makes a user say, "That actually understood me"?
Emotional Accuracy: The ability to tune for pacing, tone, and safety rather than just speed or utility.
The "Unknowns": You should be comfortable helping the founder navigate the boundary of what current AI can (and cannot) realistically achieve for an MVP.
Project tech stack
React Native (Expo), iOS, Supabase or similar, OpenAI or similar LLMs, Reanimated (for animations), TestFlight (for beta), lightweight backend, focus on prompt engineering and conversational state management.
